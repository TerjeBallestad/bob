## When to use

- Once, at the root of the app, holding every [Toast](?path=/docs/feedback-toast--docs).

## When not to use

- Inside a scrolling panel or a modal. It is fixed to the window.

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Render one ToastStack and keep it mounted, also when it is empty.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Render a stack per screen. Toasts from two stacks overlap.

</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

ToastStack is a plain `<div>`. Keep it mounted: when it becomes a live region (see Toast's gaps),
a screen reader will hear only the changes to a region that was already on the page.
