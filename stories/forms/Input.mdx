## When to use

- For short text: a name, a title, an id.
- In a [FormRow](?path=/docs/forms-formrow--docs), which puts its label above it.

## When not to use

- For text over one line. Use [Textarea](?path=/docs/forms-textarea--docs).
- To filter or search a list. Use [SearchInput](?path=/docs/forms-searchinput--docs).
- For a choice from a known list. Use [Select](?path=/docs/forms-select--docs).

## Props

<Controls of={Stories.Default} />

Input takes every attribute of `<input>`: `value` and `onChange` for a controlled field,
`defaultValue` for an uncontrolled one, and `name`, `required`, `autoFocus`.

## States

- **Focus:** the border turns indigo (`--accent-deep`).
- **Disabled:** the native attribute; the field leaves the tab order.

## Accessibility

<Keys rows={[
  ['Tab', 'Moves focus into the field.'],
]} />

- The field needs a name. A `<label>` linked by `htmlFor` and `id`, or an `aria-label`.

- Focus shows Bob's focus ring (2px, in the accent) and the accent border.
- Error state: set `aria-invalid="true"`. The border turns `danger`, and a screen reader says the
  field is invalid. Put the message in text next to it.
