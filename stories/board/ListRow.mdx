## When to use

- For lists of records where people scan ids and titles: search results, decisions, a backlog.

## When not to use

- On a board column, where state and kind matter. Use [TicketCard](?path=/docs/board-ticketcard--docs).
- For navigation. Use [NavRow](?path=/docs/board-navrow--docs).

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Keep `meta` to one Chip or a few words. The title truncates first.
- Use `done` for closed records you still show; it strikes the title and dims the row.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Give rows in one list different `idColor`s without a meaning for each.

</Dont>
</DoDont>

## In a list

A done row is struck through and dimmed.

## Accessibility

- The row is a `<div>`: a screen reader reads id, title, meta and age in that order.

- With `onClick` the row is a button (`role="button"`): Tab reaches it, Enter and Space click it,
  and it shows the focus ring. Without `onClick` it stays a plain `<div>`.

<Gap>With `onClick` the row is a button, so it cannot open in a new tab. A row for a record with its own address should be a link.</Gap>
